The problem

Buying a Book Shouldn’t Be a Gamble.

You find a great cover and a promising description. A few chapters in, you run into content you never signed up for, or a story that just doesn’t hold you. Either way, your money and your evening are gone.

  • Unwanted surprises

    Explicit scenes or a wall of profanity show up with no warning on the cover or in the description.

  • Too little to go on

    A short blurb and a few pages rarely tell you whether you’ll actually enjoy the story.

  • Money and time wasted

    A book that isn’t right for you ends up half-read on the shelf.
