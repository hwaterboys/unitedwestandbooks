Our philosophy

Exciting Storytelling. Nothing You Have to Skip.

Great fiction runs on tension, heart and stakes, not shock value. We look for stories that keep you up past midnight because you have to know what happens next.

That means romance, thrillers, adventures, science fiction without graphic sexual content, profanity or vulgarity. You get the story and leave out the trash.

Two promises, clearly defined

What You Can Count On

A promise is only useful if it’s specific. Here’s exactly what ours mean.

Preview Before Buying

Read multiple complete sample chapters on our site before you pay anything. Previews are:

  • Free, with no account or email required
  • The author’s actual text, not a summary
  • Several chapters per book; the exact number varies by title
See how previews work

Guaranteed Clean

Every book is reviewed by a person against our written, versioned standards before it can be listed. Clean means:

  • No graphic sexual content
  • No profanity
  • No vulgarity
  • No graphic violence
Read the full Clean-Content Standards

Clean isn’t the same as shallow

Real Stories Can Still Deal with Hard Things.

Clean doesn’t mean a book avoids difficult subjects. Danger, grief, war and difficult relationships are part of great storytelling, and they may appear when they serve the story.

When they do, the content is kept clean.

Not in our books

  • Graphic sexual content
  • Profanity
  • Vulgarity
  • Graphic violence
